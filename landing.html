What it does

Everything a shop counter needs, nothing it doesn't.

Inventory tracking

Stock levels, cost and sell price, suppliers, and low-stock alerts — all in one place.

Point of sale with barcode scan

Ring up sales fast — scan a barcode or search by name, right from your phone's camera.

Owner & cashier roles

Invite staff by email, control what they can see and do, and keep an eye on every account.

Works offline

No signal, no problem — sales keep working and sync automatically once you're back online.

Sales & profit reports

See what's selling, what's not, and exactly how much you're making — by day, item, or category.

One shop, every device

Your data syncs across every phone or tablet your team signs in with — always up to date.
